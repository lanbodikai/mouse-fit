## Wooting 60HE v2 ANSI assembly

The Hall Effect keyboard visualizer fetches and renders the unmodified
`wooting_60he-v2_ansi_asm_260118.stp` reference assembly from the
[Wooting design repository](https://github.com/WootingKb/wooting-design/tree/main/wooting-60he-v2).
It is converted to a GLB preset for browser performance; the corresponding
source, notices, and license are available at that URL and in the repository's

## Gateron Low Profile Magnetic Jade Mini switch models

The visualizer includes four user-supplied Gateron STEP assets: the KS-33C-FB
Low Profile Magnetic Jade Mini, KS-33D-FB Full POM Magnetic Jade Pro Mini,
KS-20T Magnetic Hall Sensor, and KS-20 Magnetic Jade. They are rendered as
Hall Effect switch presets without modifying the source geometry. Copyright
and any applicable model-use terms remain with Gateron.

## User-supplied Hall Effect keyboard shells

The visualizer includes the user-supplied `3D Printed Keyboard case for Wooting
60HE.3mf` as the case shell for the Wooting 60HE-compatible assembly. The
supplied GT60/Wooting 60HE STL archive is retained with the project assets for
future shell variants; its geometry does not match the reference assembly's
footprint closely enough to become the default shell. Copyright and any
applicable model-use terms remain with their respective creators.

## Cherry MX keycaps

The keyboard view uses selected Cherry-profile keycap meshes from
[endeavoursc/cherry-mx-keycaps](https://github.com/endeavoursc/cherry-mx-keycaps),
converted from the supplied FBX library into a compact browser preset. The
source library includes profile-specific keycaps, MX stems, and internal ribs,
and is licensed under the [MIT License](https://github.com/endeavoursc/cherry-mx-keycaps/blob/main/LICENSE).
